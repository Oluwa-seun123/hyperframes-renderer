Cinematic Director

Turn cinematography choices into executable shot instructions. Recipes are adapted from the studio's Cinematic Techniques Reference: each shot specifies what the camera does, what changes in the frame, and what must remain readable. Build a shot plan, then export HFX Scene Language.

01 · Design a shot

Physical truth rule: a still image can support crop, scale, pan, simulated focus and layered parallax. It cannot provide a real orbit, turntable rotation or new unseen surfaces. Those techniques are flagged as requiring a 3D model or additional real views.
