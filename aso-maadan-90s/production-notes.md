## Audio handoff
The current project intentionally has no audio. The suggested narration above is draft copy, not an approved brand script. At 90 seconds, target roughly 115–135 spoken words with pauses, then place licensed music underneath in your editing app. Keep voiceover, music, and sound effects on separate tracks.
