# Megamix yoghurt advert — creative brief

**Status:** Pre-production approved for development; not yet a rendered final advert  
**Target runtime:** 60 seconds  
**Master format:** 9:16 vertical, 1080×1920, 30 fps, H.264 MP4  
**Audio:** Silent master; owner may add voiceover and music  
**Budget ceiling:** $0

## Objective
Create a polished, appetising, premium-feeling Megamix yoghurt advert that looks like a real commercial rather than a slideshow. Use genuine moving footage for food preparation, fruit, yoghurt texture and serving moments. Use the owner's original Megamix product photographs for all recognisable packaging and brand reveals.

## Audience and tone
Broad consumer audience. Warm, inviting, fresh, confident and contemporary. Keep the language simple and sensory. Avoid medical, nutritional, probiotic, weight-loss or other performance claims unless the owner supplies verifiable approved wording.

## Creative idea
**“A little goodness in every moment.”** The film moves from fresh ingredients and creamy texture into everyday serving moments, then lands on a clear Megamix product reveal. The concept is a creative direction, not a factual ingredient claim.

## Story arc
1. **Hook (0–5s):** A beautiful macro food shot immediately catches attention.
2. **Sensory build (5–19s):** Fresh fruit, yoghurt being spooned or poured, close-up texture and serving preparation.
3. **Brand reveal (19–30s):** The actual Megamix range appears clearly; use original product photos, never AI-redrawn labels.
4. **Serving moment (30–45s):** Show yoghurt served in a bowl or alongside breakfast/fruit. Keep people and settings natural and inclusive.
5. **Product close (45–60s):** Return to the actual product range, show the Greek yoghurt tub where relevant, and finish on the logo and concise call to action.

## Brand/product truth rules
- Use only the product photographs supplied by the owner as the source of truth for bottle/tub design, logo, label, colours and product variants.
- Do not invent ingredients, certifications, nutritional facts, health benefits, prices, sizes, stockists or availability.
- Do not imply that stock-footage people endorse Megamix.
- Any external footage must have a recorded source URL, asset identifier, licence and commercial-use check.
- Avoid shots that visually imply an ingredient is in the product unless confirmed by the owner. Fruit can be used as a serving suggestion, not as an ingredient claim.
- Product labels must be legible and unaltered in the final composite.

## Visual direction
- Photorealistic live-action footage, natural window light, shallow depth of field, macro texture, controlled camera movement.
- Cream, warm white and restrained berry tones; use the actual brand palette sampled from supplied assets during design.
- Clean typography with generous negative space; no excessive stickers, fake UI or template-like transitions.
- Use motion graphics to support real footage, not replace it.

## Deliverables
- 60-second vertical silent master MP4.
- 15-second cutdown only after the 60-second master is approved.
- Poster frame and a ZIP package containing the MP4, poster, project notes and asset/licence manifest.
- Include a clear note identifying any temporary placeholder footage.

## Production gates
- Gate 1: Owner approves creative direction and any product claims.
- Gate 2: All footage sources and licences are logged.
- Gate 3: Scene assembly and brand/product accuracy review.
- Gate 4: Render validation (duration, dimensions, frame rate, playback, black frames, audio policy, file size).
- Gate 5: ZIP is created and tested before sharing.
