# Photoreal production brief — THE NIGHT HAS NO LIMITS

## Locked creative decisions
- Device: Samsung Galaxy S26 Ultra, Cobalt Violet
- Direction: premium cinematic realism; not sci-fi illustration
- Master: 30 seconds, 9:16, 1080x1920, 30 fps; six 5-second scenes
- No people, no voiceover
- Existing HyperFrames project remains the timing, typography, transitions and assembly layer.

## Product truth and reference policy
Use Samsung's official Galaxy S26 Ultra Cobalt Violet product imagery as the visual truth source. Reference page: https://www.samsungmobilepress.com/media-assets/galaxy-s26-ultra?category=Product
Official product page: https://www.samsung.com/us/smartphones/galaxy-s26-ultra/

Required reference views: rear three-quarter, straight rear, front, left/right side, camera-island close-up, and a clean isolated packshot if available. Prefer the highest-resolution official files. Keep a copy of the original, source page, date obtained, and any published usage terms with the asset record.

Do not ask an image model to invent the phone, camera array, Samsung wordmark, S Pen, UI, or product specifications. AI may create environments and abstract optical plates, but the final product must be composited from official imagery or a verified 3D asset. Never redraw or distort the Samsung logo. Do not imply Samsung sponsorship or endorsement.

## Asset delivery standard
- Preferred generated/source plates: 2160x3840 or higher, vertical 9:16; 1080x1920 minimum for final use.
- Motion plates: 5–7 seconds, 24/25/30 fps, no baked-in text, no logos, no watermarks, no abrupt cuts, no camera shake unless explicitly called for.
- Still plates: high-detail, clean composition with room for title copy; use subtle parallax only when it does not invent geometry.
- Scene plate should be graded consistently: deep clean blacks, cool cyan-white highlights, restrained violet accents, natural highlight rolloff, no excessive bloom.
- Keep important subject details within a 8% side safe margin and 10% top/bottom safe margin.
- Avoid repeated footage. Each scene must have its own distinct source plate.

## Scene 01 — First Light (00:00–00:05)
Create a photoreal extreme macro of precision optical glass, abstract and unbranded. Concentric coated-glass rings catch a narrow cyan-white light sweep from left to right. Shallow depth of field, realistic micro-scratches and dust only if subtle, physically plausible reflections, black studio environment, macro cinema lens, slow controlled push-in. No camera body, no phone, no text, no logos, no visible fabricated hardware diagram. End on a bright circular glint positioned near centre to match Scene 02.

## Scene 02 — Enter the Night (00:05–00:10)
A photoreal premium night city viewed through a circular optical opening. Distinct foreground, middle-distance and distant skyscrapers; wet dark street or waterfront with broken natural reflections; thin atmospheric haze; restrained cyan-white practical lights; realistic exposure and lens response. Slow forward dolly, subtle parallax, no flying cars, no fantasy architecture, no people, no text, no logos. A single tower light should land near the hero droplet highlight used in Scene 03.

## Scene 03 — What Others Miss (00:10–00:15)
Extreme macro of one physically believable spherical raindrop on dark glass. Inside the droplet, a real-looking night skyline is inverted and optically compressed by refraction. Hero droplet sharp, surrounding tiny droplets softly out of focus; controlled specular highlight; shallow depth of field; slow precise focus pull. No text, no logos, no phone, no impossible liquid motion. End with the droplet highlight expanding to match a glass optical element in Scene 04.

## Scene 04 — Inside the Lens (00:15–00:20)
A conceptual, non-literal optical study: several transparent curved glass elements spaced along one clean horizontal optical axis in a black studio. A narrow cyan-white beam passes through each element and converges to a point; realistic caustics, internal reflections and subtle restrained violet edge reflections. Slow three-quarter camera slide. Do not depict or label actual Galaxy internals; no claims of a verified teardown, no phone, no text, no logos. End with the converging beam flattening into a luminous horizon line for Scene 05.

## Scene 05 — World Revealed (00:20–00:25)
A photoreal cinematic vertical night-city panorama, preferably waterfront or elevated rooftop viewpoint. Strong foreground depth, layered midground buildings and distant skyline; one luminous central tower; subtle atmospheric perspective; wet surface or water reflecting lights naturally. Slow crane rise or controlled pullback, no people, no fantasy neon overload, no text, no logos. A restrained cyan light streak moves toward camera and becomes the studio rim light in Scene 06.

## Scene 06 — Hero Reveal (00:25–00:30)
Use the official Galaxy S26 Ultra Cobalt Violet product image or verified 3D model as the exact device source. Place it in a premium midnight-black studio with a dark reflective surface, soft controlled key light, cool silver edge light and a restrained violet rim. Slow camera orbit or controlled parallax only if the source/model supports it. Preserve exact camera layout, translucent camera-island design, frame, proportions, finish and button positions. No invented lenses, no altered branding, no fake UI, no extra device. Add approved Samsung logo and Galaxy S26 Ultra title as separate crisp graphic layers, not generated inside the image. Hold final end card for at least 1.5 seconds.

## Transition and edit rules
1→2: central lens glint becomes circular city portal.
2→3: tower practical light match-cuts to droplet highlight.
3→4: droplet highlight expands into the first optical glass element.
4→5: converging beam stretches into city horizon.
5→6: cyan streak curves into product studio rim light.
Use short, invisible match transitions; no generic flashy wipes. Retain the existing scene timings and typography until visual approval.

## Audio brief
Original instrumental cinematic electronic score, no vocals and no voiceover. Start with near-silent low-frequency atmosphere, build a restrained pulse through scenes 2–4, open into a wide cinematic lift in scene 5, then land a confident low impact and delicate high-frequency shimmer on the hero reveal. Add original designed glass resonance, city air, rain micro-detail, optical sweep and final product reveal impact. Record licence/provenance for every audio element. Target approximately -14 LUFS integrated and no higher than -1 dBTP true peak.

## Approval gates
- Gate A: product references are official and usage permission/terms are understood.
- Gate B: six distinct visual plates reviewed as still frames before animation.
- Gate C: motion and transitions reviewed in a low-resolution assembly.
- Gate D: product accuracy, typography, colour, audio and legal/brand review.
- Gate E: final 1080x1920/30fps MP4 validated and ZIP packaged.

Do not label the deliverable a photoreal final commercial until the actual photographic/3D assets are present and all gates pass.
