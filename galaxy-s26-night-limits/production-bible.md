# Production bible — The Night Has No Limits

## Master
- 30 seconds; six five-second scenes
- 9:16 vertical, 1080 × 1920, 30 fps
- No humans; no voiceover
- Premium cinematic hybrid: photoreal target visuals plus construction sketches as spatial guidance
- Palette: midnight blue, black glass, cool silver, cyan, restrained violet

## Scene 01 — First Light (00:00–00:05)
Extreme macro of an abstract dark precision-lens surface. Concentric rings emerge from near-black. A narrow cyan-white streak travels left to right and ignites a central glint. Keep the optics abstract; do not imply verified internal construction. Copy: “THE NIGHT”. Match the illuminated centre to Scene 02's circular city portal.

## Scene 02 — Enter the Night (00:05–00:10)
A believable night skyline appears inside the circular opening. Layer near buildings, midground towers and distant skyscrapers; use wet streets or water for broken reflections. Smooth forward dolly and parallax. Copy: “HAS NO” then “LIMITS”. Match a tower light to the droplet highlight in Scene 03.

## Scene 03 — What Others Miss (00:10–00:15)
One spherical raindrop rests on a dark reflective surface. The city appears inverted and compressed inside it by refraction. Surrounding droplets are soft bokeh; hero droplet is crisp. Copy: “WHAT / OTHERS / MISS.” Expand its highlight into the glass element of Scene 04. This is visual metaphor, not a technical teardown claim.

## Scene 04 — Inside the Lens (00:15–00:20)
Conceptual exploded optical assembly: transparent curved glass elements spaced along one horizontal optical axis. A narrow cyan beam passes through and converges to a point. Three-quarter side view, restrained violet reflections. Copy: “PRECISION” then “IN EVERY DETAIL”. Reassemble the beam into a horizon line for Scene 05. This is illustrative, not a verified Galaxy S26 Ultra component diagram.

## Scene 05 — World Revealed (00:20–00:25)
Wide vertical night-city panorama with foreground rooftops or waterfront, midground skyline and distant towers. A luminous central tower anchors the composition; water or wet ground carries subtle reflections. Copy: “SEE THE UNSEEN.” A cyan streak travels toward camera and curves into the studio rim light around the phone in Scene 06.

## Scene 06 — Hero Reveal (00:25–00:30)
Premium Galaxy S26 Ultra hero shot on a midnight studio background. Use a verified model/reference for exact rear silhouette, camera layout, frame, finish and proportions. Official Samsung logo artwork plus “Galaxy S26 Ultra”; never redraw the logo. Copy: “THE NIGHT HAS NO LIMITS.” Hold final end card at least 1.5 seconds. No outgoing transition.

## Global execution rules
- One deterministic timeline; visual states derive from absolute time.
- No timers, unseeded randomness, or state dependent on previous frames.
- Use smooth ease-in-out curves; keep text and logos crisp.
- Typography: one modern sans-serif family, at most two weights.
- Keep copy at least 8% inset from left/right and 10% from top/bottom.
- Coherent light direction, plausible reflections, restrained bloom, clean blacks.
- Technical pass: 30-second runtime, 1080 × 1920, 30 fps, six scenes, no black frames, no clipped text, readable end card.
- Audio: original cinematic electronic score and designed effects only; no voiceover. Target around -14 LUFS integrated and no higher than -1 dBTP true peak.
